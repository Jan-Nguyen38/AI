# Lumen Robotics Employee Handbook (sample)

This is a fictional handbook used to demo the RAG chatbot.

## Working hours and remote work

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees choose their own schedule.
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from a department head.
The Hanoi and Berlin offices are open from 07:00 to 21:00 on weekdays.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid vacation per year, plus public holidays in their country.
Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.
Sick leave is unlimited for short illnesses, but absences longer than 3 consecutive days need a doctor's note.
Parental leave is 20 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Equipment

New employees choose a laptop: a 14-inch MacBook Pro or a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
Each employee gets a one-time home office budget of 600 EUR for a desk, chair, monitor or similar.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a yearly learning budget of 1,500 EUR for courses, books and conferences.
Requests over 500 EUR need manager approval in the HR portal before purchase.

## Expenses

Submit expenses within 30 days through the Expensify app with a photo of the receipt.
Meals during business travel are covered up to 60 EUR per day. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.
